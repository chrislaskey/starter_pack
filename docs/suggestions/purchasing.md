# Purchasing: what was left out

Built so far: Stripe on the web and the desktop
(`docs/design/purchasing.md`), the App Store in the iPhone app and
deleting an account (`docs/design/purchasing-ios-build.md`). Left for
later:

- **Linking to the web checkout from a US app.** Under the Epic
  injunctions a US app may link out to its own checkout. Checked
  2026-11-12: Apple charges nothing on those links today, but the fee
  is still before the courts, and Google means to charge one. When it
  settles: a "Buy on the web" link on a plan's page in the US
  storefront, opening Stripe's checkout in the browser as the desktop
  does, beside the App Store's sheet. See "The US is the exception" in
  `docs/design/purchasing-phones.md`.
- **Exporting one's data.** Before deleting an account, or at any
  time: the person's posts, files and communities as a download, made
  by a job and mailed as a link. GDPR's right to portability asks for
  it on request; the privacy page says to email for it until then.

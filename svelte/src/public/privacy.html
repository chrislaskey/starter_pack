This policy explains what [Company name] ("we", "us") collects when you use Starter Pack on the web, the desktop or a phone, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

1. What we collect

  • Your account: your name, your email address, and a password if you set one (stored only as a secure hash). We email you codes to sign in and to confirm changes.

  • On your devices: the apps keep a copy of your content so they work offline, and data you keep in an app before signing up stays on that device and is never sent to us.

2. Why we use it

  • To run the service: your account, signing in, syncing your content between your devices and with the people you share it with (performing our contract with you).
  • To take payments and provide what you bought (contract).
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems (our legitimate interests).
  • To email you about your account, such as sign-in codes, purchases and changes to these policies (contract and legitimate interests). We do not send marketing email without your consent.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as tax and accounting records.

  • Apple, which takes payments in the iPhone and iPad app through the App Store. Apple tells us about the purchase, with an id that links it to your account, not your Apple ID's details.

Your content is also visible to the members of the communities you post in. We disclose data when the law requires it, and to a buyer of our business, who would be bound by this policy.

4. Cookies and browser storage

On the web we use one cookie, to keep you signed in. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser also keeps a few settings of yours, such as your theme and whether you are signed in, in its local storage, which never leaves your device.
